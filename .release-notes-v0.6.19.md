# v0.6.19 — Phone comment chips stay tappable and clear of the box

Patch on top of [v0.6.18](https://github.com/roxelfivan/apeditor/releases/tag/v0.6.18). The Send to AI numbered chips on a phone sit in the centre of the bar, each chip opens its own comment, and the comment box sits above that bar instead of covering it.

## Highlights

- **Centred chips.** Comment 1 / 2 / 3 sit in the middle of the Send to AI bar, not on the left and right edges.
- **Tap the chip you mean.** Opening 1 or 2 no longer jumps to the last comment. Each chip owns its hit box.
- **Box above the bar.** The phone comment sheet docks in the write pane, 8px above the chips, so the box and the bar do not overlap.

No new Settings. No migration.

## Verification

- `npm run typecheck` — clean.
- `npm run test:mobile-shell` — pass.
